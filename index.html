Good women.
Brighter days.

Between the school run, the business, the messages that never stop — a whole day that asks nothing of you except that you show up.

We take over a beach hotel for one Sunday. You move, you eat well, you get looked after, you meet women who are building something too. Then the sun goes down and we stay for the moon.

Small on purpose. Private on purpose.

The day

Eight moments, from first light to full moon.

Everything runs at the pace of the beach. Join what calls you, skip what doesn't.

Morning

Yoga

An opening flow on the sand, while the bay is still quiet and the light is still soft.

Aquagym

Straight into the water. Low impact, high laughter — the warm-up nobody expects to enjoy.

Midday

Healthy lunch

A long table in the shade. Fresh fish, island produce, nothing that slows the afternoon down.

Vegan smoothies

Mango, passion fruit, coconut, ginger — pulled from the bar all day long.

Afternoon

Beauty & massages

Hands-on time with the team: treatments and massage, booked in turns through the afternoon.

Personal training

A coach on the sand for whoever wants to work — one on one, at your level, no audience.

Golden hour

Aperitivo

Feet in the sand, glass in hand, great music. The part of the day where the real conversations start.

Full moon ritual

We close together on the beach under the September moon — the reason the date is the date.
